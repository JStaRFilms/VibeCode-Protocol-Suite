# Zack 3D Style Reference

## STYLE FORMULA

Glossy stylized-realistic 3D animation, physically coherent proportions with
slightly rounded forms, clean PBR materials, crisp bevels, warm golden sunlight,
cobalt-blue sky, soft cinematic fill, saturated but controlled color, clear
silhouette separation, shallow depth of field on close shots, polished educational
short-form render, energetic camera perspective, high detail without visual clutter.

Copy the STYLE FORMULA byte-identically into every style-key, asset-sheet, and
animation prompt. Add subject-specific content after it; never rewrite the formula
mid-project.

## Annotation grammar

- Neon green glow, outline, arrow, or rectangle: reveal, focus, mechanism.
- Red dashed trajectory, crack, impact burst, or danger highlight: force, danger,
  failure, collision, or destructive physics.
- Shapes only; no generated words.
