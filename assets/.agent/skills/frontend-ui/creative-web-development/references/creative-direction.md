# Creative Direction from Sparse Briefs

Use this workflow when the user supplies a subject but leaves the identity, experience, or visual direction open. The output is a selected concept and experience architecture, not an implementation inventory.

## 1. Build the Truth Ledger

Record three categories before ideating:

| Category | Meaning | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Known** | Explicitly supplied or verified facts | Use directly. |
| **Provisional** | Reasonable working assumptions needed to plan | Label and keep easy to replace. |
| **Unknown** | Facts requiring the user, research, or a real data source | Preserve as questions or content requirements. |

Invent fictional details only when the user explicitly requests a fictional brand or grants permission to do so. Ambiguity is permission to make design decisions, not permission to manufacture business facts.

The classification must survive beyond this ledger. If a provisional detail is needed to demonstrate the experience, mark it where it appears. Do not convert a provisional service, price, address, schedule, amenity, integration, or capability into ordinary customer-facing copy later in the plan. Unknowns remain requirements or placeholders.

### Completion Gate

- [ ] Every factual claim is known, provisional, or unknown.
- [ ] Operational details remain labelled at their point of use, not only in the ledger.
- [ ] Unknown operational details remain visible in the plan.

## 2. Excavate the Domain

Look beneath the category name. Extract material from at least five lenses:

1. **Functional truth**: What work is actually performed? What changes state?
2. **Visitor reality**: Why does someone arrive, what uncertainty do they carry, and what must they accomplish quickly?
3. **Human ritual**: What repeated behavior, emotion, social exchange, or private moment surrounds the subject?
4. **Material behavior**: What physical properties, rhythms, sounds, traces, tools, environments, or transformations belong to it?
5. **Cultural tension**: What expectation, stigma, contradiction, overlooked value, or changing convention makes it interesting now?
6. **Native language**: What verbs, phrases, measurements, labels, and signs belong specifically to this world?

Separate useful domain truth from visual decoration. A shape associated with the subject is not yet a concept.

### Completion Gate

- [ ] Visitor jobs and a meaningful domain tension are identified.
- [ ] At least three subject-specific behaviors or materials could influence the experience.
- [ ] The findings would not apply unchanged to an unrelated category.

## 3. Quarantine the First Association

Name the most immediate visual metaphor, expected layout, and common aesthetic treatment. Keep them available as raw material, but prevent them from becoming the answer before alternatives exist.

The first association may return only if it is transformed by a deeper human or domain insight. Literal relevance alone is insufficient.

### Completion Gate

- [ ] The obvious concept is visible rather than unconsciously controlling the process.
- [ ] At least two later candidates do not rely on it.

## 4. Diverge Across Concept Families

Generate three to five candidates that differ in governing premise and experience structure, not merely palette or effects. Span at least three of these lenses:

- **Material**: Begin with a native physical process or behavior.
- **Ritual**: Begin with the human experience, habit, or emotion.
- **System**: Begin with relationships, flows, timing, exchange, or transformation.
- **Place**: Begin with architecture, neighborhood, environment, or spatial memory.
- **Editorial**: Begin with language, evidence, curation, or a strong point of view.
- **Contrarian**: Challenge the category's expected tone or interaction model while protecting utility.

For each candidate, define:

1. **Governing proposition**: One sentence describing what the website fundamentally is.
2. **Domain truth**: The observation that earns the proposition.
3. **Visitor promise**: What the experience helps someone feel, understand, or do.
4. **Experience structure**: Utility, editorial, narrative, product, spatial, or hybrid.
5. **Visual world**: Composition, typography, imagery, material, and color logic.
6. **Motion law**: One rule describing how the world responds or changes.
7. **Signature interaction**: One interaction that expresses the proposition.
8. **Utility integration**: How essential actions remain direct.
9. **Ownability test**: Why this direction belongs to this subject rather than any fashionable website.

Keep libraries, shader types, frameworks, and production recipes out of candidate generation. Technical availability should not choose the idea.

### Completion Gate

- [ ] Candidates differ in premise, structure, and interaction grammar.
- [ ] At least two candidates escape the first association.
- [ ] Every candidate preserves essential visitor utility.

## 5. Select with Evidence

Evaluate candidates using [concept-evaluation.md](concept-evaluation.md). Reject candidates that fail factual integrity or visitor utility regardless of spectacle.

Choose the direction with the strongest combination of domain truth, ownability, coherence, interaction necessity, and feasible ambition. Give the user a concise decision summary: why the winner is strongest, what was rejected, and what risk remains. Do not expose private chain-of-thought or pretend the choice is objective certainty.

Use numeric scoring comparatively, not ceremonially. A score of 5 requires exceptional evidence against that dimension's definition. A perfect or near-perfect winner is a signal to stress-test again, not proof that the concept is finished. Record at least one substantive weakness or unresolved creative risk for the selected direction.

### Completion Gate

- [ ] The selected concept passes every hard gate.
- [ ] The selection rationale cites observable properties of the candidates.
- [ ] The winner retains a substantive weakness or risk rather than being declared flawless.
- [ ] Rejected directions are not silently blended back into the winner.

## 6. Write the Concept Contract

Define the selected direction before storyboarding or implementation:

| Field | Required decision |
|---|---|
| **Concept** | One memorable governing sentence |
| **Experience promise** | Intended visitor understanding, feeling, and action |
| **Visitor jobs** | Primary tasks and persistent utility |
| **Structure** | Utility, editorial, narrative, product, spatial, or hybrid |
| **Composition** | Scale, density, rhythm, grid, framing, and whitespace |
| **Typography** | Voice, hierarchy, behavior, and legibility |
| **Material and color** | Subject-derived visual logic rather than a fashionable palette |
| **Imagery** | Required subject matter, treatment, and provenance |
| **Motion law** | How elements enter, respond, transform, and settle |
| **Signature interaction** | Trigger, behavior, meaning, and recovery state |
| **Sound** | Purpose and opt-in behavior, or explicit omission |
| **Responsive expression** | How touch and small screens express the same idea |
| **Accessibility expression** | Keyboard, reduced motion, semantics, and equivalent access |
| **Restraint** | Attractive ideas and technologies intentionally excluded |
| **Unknowns** | Facts, assets, and integrations still requiring confirmation |

The contract should make later design choices easier to judge. If two proposed treatments conflict, prefer the one that better expresses the contract.

## 7. Calibrate Ambition

Increase ambition by deepening the relationship between idea, content, interaction, and visitor consequence:

- Make the premise more precise and ownable.
- Let the interface behavior reveal meaning rather than decorate content.
- Create stronger state changes, pacing, or participation when the concept earns them.
- Resolve mobile, accessibility, sound, and utility as intentional expressions of the same idea.
- Improve craft, responsiveness, and continuity.

Technical complexity is justified only after these dimensions are strong.

For plan-only work, stop at experiential decisions. Do not load technical references or add stacks, formulas, runtime architecture, budgets, or implementation phases to perform ambition.

### Final Gate

- [ ] The direction is memorable without naming a technology.
- [ ] The signature interaction would lose meaning if transferred to another subject.
- [ ] The plan distinguishes facts from invented creative decisions.
- [ ] Provisional operational details remain labelled everywhere they appear.
- [ ] The experience remains useful before enhanced effects load.
- [ ] A plan-only deliverable contains no technical blueprint.
- [ ] The concept contract is complete enough to guide implementation.
