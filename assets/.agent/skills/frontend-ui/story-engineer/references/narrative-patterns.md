# Narrative Patterns & Templates

Reference for constructing narrative rails, identifying contradictions, and balancing emotional registers. Consult during **Stage 3**, **Stage 4**, and **Stage 5**.

---

## 1. Contradiction Archetypes

Look for these recurring tensions in technical and creative projects:

| Contradiction Archetype | Core Tension | Driving Question Example |
|---|---|---|
| **The Inverted Tool** | Using the very technology causing a problem to solve it. | *"Can we use AI to escape AI-looking aesthetics?"* |
| **The Anachronism** | Combining ancient/historical rigor with futuristic machinery. | *"What happens when 1970s medical expert systems drive modern WebGL shaders?"* |
| **The Discipline Paradox** | Imposing hyper-strict rules to achieve total artistic freedom. | *"Does extreme constraint produce wilder creativity?"* |
| **The Scale Inversion** | Squeezing massive complexity into a single, microscopic artifact. | *"How do 750 pages of theory become a single fluid interaction?"* |
| **The Ghost in the Rig** | Giving mathematical determinism an organic, living soul. | *"Where does math end and feeling begin?"* |

## 2. Emotional Modulation Palette

Use this matrix to ensure adjacent beats alternate in emotional frequency:

```
[ WONDER ]
   │
   ▼ (shifts from open curiosity to deep focus)
[ TECHNICAL CURIOSITY / DECONSTRUCTION ]
   │
   ▼ (shifts from mechanics to massive volume)
[ SCALE & MONUMENTAL WEIGHT ]
   │
   ▼ (shifts from heavy data to competitive tension)
[ SUSPENSE / THE TRIAL ]
   │
   ▼ (shifts from tension to honesty)
[ VULNERABILITY / BREAKDOWN ]
   │
   ▼ (shifts from failure to triumphant release)
[ EXPLOSION / THE REVEAL ]
   │
   ▼ (shifts from spectacle to forward-looking wonder)
[ MYSTERY & HORIZON ]
```

## 3. The 6-Beat Narrative Rail Template

Use this canonical rail for case studies, product launches, and interactive web experiences:

```markdown
### Beat 1: The Inciting Question
- **Goal**: Hook the visitor with an unresolved paradox.
- **Register**: `WONDER`
- **Copy Focus**: The Driving Question + the uncomfortable reality.
- **Generates**: *"What caused this, and why has no one fixed it?"*

### Beat 2: The Deconstruction
- **Goal**: Prove the phenomenon is not magic, but systems.
- **Register**: `TECHNICAL CURIOSITY`
- **Copy Focus**: Breaking the problem into core components.
- **Generates**: *"Where do we find the deep expertise to master these systems?"*

### Beat 3: The Gathering (Heavy Knowledge)
- **Goal**: Establish staggering scale, historical depth, and authority.
- **Register**: `SCALE & WEIGHT`
- **Copy Focus**: Massive documentation, formal methodologies, raw volume.
- **Generates**: *"How do you forge all this volume into a singular capability?"*

### Beat 4: The Crucible (Synthesis & Crisis)
- **Goal**: Show the collision of models, competition, or unexpected breakdown.
- **Register**: `SUSPENSE` into `VULNERABILITY`
- **Copy Focus**: The tournament, the failed build, the critical bug, the turning point.
- **Generates**: *"Did the experiment survive? What actually emerged?"*

### Beat 5: The Reveal (The Climax)
- **Goal**: Deliver the uncompromised artifact or breakthrough.
- **Register**: `EXPLOSION`
- **Copy Focus**: The artifact itself (full sensory impact, interactive demo).
- **Generates**: *"What does this mean for the future?"*

### Beat 6: The Horizon
- **Goal**: Point outward to the paradigm shift.
- **Register**: `MYSTERY / HORIZON`
- **Copy Focus**: Open frontier, invitation to explore.
```
